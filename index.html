Coming next: Custom Fit

Go beyond choosing a size

FitCheck helps you choose the closest existing size. Custom Fit will go further by adapting a pattern you already own to your actual body measurements, gauge and preferred fit.

FitCheck

“Which existing size should I knit?”

Custom Fit

“How should I modify this pattern so it fits me?”

What Custom Fit will do

  • Body circumference Adjusted to your measurements and the ease you want.
  • Body and sleeve lengths Set to where you want your sweater to end.
  • Sleeve shaping Adapted where the pattern’s construction allows it.
  • Instructions for your pattern Specific changes that refer to the pattern you already own.

Custom Fit is being developed for supported garment constructions, starting with top-down raglan pullovers. When a safe fit recommendation can’t be made for a pattern, it will tell you so instead of guessing.
